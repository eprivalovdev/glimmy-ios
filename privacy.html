Effective date: November 24, 2026

Short version: Glimmy collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your breaks and settings stay on your iPhone. If you allow the camera, Glimmy looks at your eyes during a break to play along, then forgets what it saw: nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

The camera and your face

Glimmy asks for the camera only when you choose to turn on eye tracking (during setup or in Settings). Breaks never ask for it, and everything works without it.

When you allow it, on iPhones with Face ID, Glimmy uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking with the TrueDepth camera while a break is on screen. Each moment it reads three things: where your eyes are pointing, how open each eye is, and how far your face is from the phone. Glimmy uses them right away to blink when you blink, to wait when you look away, to count the sparks your eyes catch and to tell you when the phone is close enough in Bring It Close.

This face data is processed on your iPhone only and is thrown away as soon as it has been used. Glimmy does not take photos or video, does not keep a face map, does not identify you and does not send any of it to the developer or to anyone else. It is never used for advertising or for any purpose other than the break you are doing.

One thing is kept, if you do the short calibration: six numbers that adjust Glimmy to how your eyes move (nothing that could recreate an image of you). It stays in Glimmy’s storage on your iPhone and is deleted with the app. You can turn the camera off at any time in iOS Settings > Glimmy.

What stays on your iPhone

Glimmy stores the breaks you finish (date, which break, how long, how many blinks it counted), your settings and whether you have Glimmy Plus. This lives in Glimmy’s own storage on your iPhone, shared only with Glimmy’s widgets through an App Group on the same device. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, this small file is part of your own backup, like any app’s data.

Reminders

Break reminders are local notifications that Glimmy schedules on your iPhone. There is no push service. You choose the rhythm and days, and you can turn reminders off in Glimmy or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Glimmy Plus subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Glimmy only asks the App Store whether your purchase is active.

Network use

Glimmy does not call any server run by the developer. The only connections it makes are to the App Store (to load prices, buy and restore purchases) and, when you tap a link, to this website.

What the developer does not do

  • Does not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.
  • Does not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Does not run a backend that stores your information.

Deleting your data

Deleting Glimmy from your iPhone removes everything it stored there, including your break history and the calibration numbers. Nothing remains with the developer, because nothing was ever sent.

Children

Glimmy is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may process standard server logs under its own privacy statement.
