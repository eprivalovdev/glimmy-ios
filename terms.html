The agreement

Glimmy is licensed to you under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (Apple’s standard EULA). These terms add a few details about Glimmy Plus and about what Glimmy is for. If they ever disagree with Apple’s EULA, the EULA wins.

Glimmy Plus

Glimmy is free to download, and its free part includes three breaks for morning, daytime and evening, 12 exercises, eye tracking, reminders, the progress calendar and widgets. Glimmy Plus adds five more breaks, the remaining exercises and your own mixes. It is offered as a yearly subscription, a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices for your country are shown in the app before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends, at the same price unless Apple tells you otherwise. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.

Free trial

The yearly plan may come with a free trial. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it turns into the paid yearly subscription and your Apple Account is charged. A free trial is available once per Apple Account; any unused part of a trial ends when you buy a subscription.

Cancel or change

Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To get Plus back on a new iPhone, use Restore purchases in Glimmy’s Settings.

Comfort, not medical care

Glimmy offers short breaks to rest your eyes from the screen. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose any condition. Go gently. Stop an exercise if anything hurts, blurs or doubles. If your eyes hurt, or your vision changes, see an eye doctor.

Using Glimmy

Please don’t do eyes-closed or look-away exercises while driving, walking or doing anything that needs your eyes. Glimmy and its artwork, text and sounds belong to the developer; you may use them for your own breaks.
