Support

How can I help?

Answers to common questions. If yours isn’t here, write to me. I read every email myself.

Do I need an iPhone with Face ID?

No. Every break works on any iPhone with iOS 26. Face ID iPhones add the playful part: Glimmy blinks with you, waits when you look away and counts the sparks you catch.

Does Glimmy record my face?

No. With your OK, Glimmy reads where your eyes point and how open they are while a break is on screen, uses it on the spot and forgets it. No photos, no video, nothing saved or sent. More about the camera.

Glimmy doesn’t blink with me. What can I do?

Check that the camera is allowed in iOS Settings > Glimmy, hold the phone at a comfortable reading distance with your face in good light, and run Calibrate my eyes in Glimmy’s Settings. Glasses that reflect a lot of light can make tracking less sure.

My reminders don’t show up.

Make sure Break reminders is on in Glimmy’s Settings and notifications are allowed in iOS Settings > Glimmy. Focus modes can hold reminders back; you can let Glimmy through in the Focus settings. Glimmy also goes quiet once you have done your three breaks for the day.

How do I hear when to open my eyes?

Soft chimes and gentle taps mark every moment you need to act. Sounds stay quiet when your iPhone is on silent, but the taps still come. You can switch either off in Settings > Break cues.

What do I get with Glimmy Plus?

Five more breaks (Deep Reset, Focus Warm-up, After Gaming, Reading Break and Blink Break) and all 24 exercises. Everything else, including eye tracking, reminders, progress and widgets, is free.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Glimmy Plus. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Plus but it’s locked on my new iPhone.

Open Settings in Glimmy and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Glimmy. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then the widget area.

Is this a treatment for my eyes?

No. Glimmy is for comfort: short breaks from the screen. If your eyes hurt or your vision changes, please see an eye doctor.
