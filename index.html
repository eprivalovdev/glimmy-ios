How a break goes

Follow, blink, rest. That's the whole trick.

Each break is a few short steps on its own pastel sky. Before every step Glimmy tells you what's next, so you never have to read while your eyes should be resting.

Step 1

Follow me

Glimmy glides in loops, eights and zigzags. Your eyes follow, your head stays still.

Step 2

Blink with me

Slow blinks, quick flutters, a gentle squeeze. Screens make us forget to blink. Glimmy reminds you.

Step 3

Rest your eyes

Palms over closed eyes, a few slow breaths. Soft chimes and gentle taps tell you when to open them.

On iPhones with Face ID

Glimmy plays along.

With your OK, the front camera lets Glimmy notice what your eyes are doing. You can always say no. Every break works without the camera too.

  • Blinks when you blinkClose your eyes and Glimmy closes its own. It's oddly delightful.
  • Waits when you look awayGlance at a message and Glimmy slows down until your eyes are back.
  • Counts what you catchIn Catch the Spark, every spark your eyes jump to counts.
  • Knows how close the phone isIn Bring It Close, Glimmy says when to stop and move the phone back.

What the camera sees stays on your iPhone.

Glimmy reads where your eyes point and how open they are, frame by frame, and then forgets it. No photos, no video, nothing saved, nothing sent. There's no account and no server.
